## What is WIN?

World Industries Network (WIN) is a global B2B platform where entrepreneurs, manufacturers and businesses find each other, trade, and run their operations from one place.

Most tools make you choose: a social network, or a marketplace, or a CRM, or an ERP. WIN is all four behind one login. The person you met in the feed becomes the supplier in your marketplace order, becomes the lead in your CRM, becomes the line in your finance ledger, without exporting anything.

**WIN is in beta.** Real accounts, real listings, real payments, real payouts. The whole interface ships in **22 languages**.

## What We Offer

### Social

- **Feed.** Four formats behind one bar: Threads (text), Hooks (short vertical video), Meets (events) and Lives (livestreams, coming soon).
- **Composer.** Photos, video, emoji, GIFs, @mentions, #hashtags, $TICKERS, scheduling and drafts.
- **Stories.** 24 hour posts with viewer insights, reactions, and private stories aimed at chosen connections. Highlights keep the ones worth keeping.
- **Chat.** Real time direct and group messaging, with categories, pinning, filters, and a separate inbox per identity. Message a seller straight from a product page.
- **Connections.** Mutual connections and one way follows, tracked separately, per identity.
- **Boost.** Promote a post, then see in Curiosity what it cost and what it returned.
- **Streaks.** Post every day and your company keeps its flame. Miss a day and it lapses, restorable through a paid restore.

### OASIS (the marketplace)

- **Listings.** Products **and services**, with photos, variants, stock, shipping rules and multi currency pricing.
- **Supply & Demand.** Post an **offer** for what you sell, or a **request** for what you need, including **requesting a service** you want someone to perform. Buyers can pull, not just sellers push.
- **Search.** Across companies, products and services, with filters, sorting and two grid modes.
- **Storefronts.** Every company gets a public page: its wall, its Team tab, its catalogue, its reviews.
- **Cart, wishlist and orders.** Multi seller carts, one payment, a separate shipment and payout per seller, promo codes, order tracking.
- **Catalogue import.** Bring an existing catalogue in by CSV instead of retyping it.

### Organization (CRM, ERP, Legal)

A full company back office, gated per permission. You only see what your role is allowed to open.

- **Team.** Org chart with drag and drop reporting lines, member list, roles, invitations.
- **Tasks.** Assign, set due dates, move across a board, with notifications and history.
- **Calendar.** A shared team calendar next to your personal one.
- **CRM.** Leads pipeline, support tickets, newsletters, and import from Salesforce or Slack.
- **ERP.** Finance, HR, projects and assets.
- **Legal & Compliance.** Contracts, compliance obligations, audits.
- **Insights.** Automatic alerts plus breakdowns for team, cash, pipeline, projects and compliance.

### Networking

- **Meets.** Events near you and online, as a list or a swipe deck.
- **Jobs.** List view or swipe view. Right to apply, left to skip.
- **CV.** Fill it once, every application reuses it, and it gets a public page you can share.
- **Postings and Talent.** Publish roles, see who applied, and search people by skill.
- **Applications.** Every job you applied to, and where it stands.

### AI

- **Jarv1s.** The in app assistant. Ask how something works, ask about your own numbers, or ask it to take you somewhere. Runs on an in house model, scoped to the workspace you are in.
- **D0nna.** The organization side assistant: scheduling, reminders, keeping work moving.
- **Curiosity.** Analytics: overview, business, engagement, social, content, boost, plus a tools section that generates QR codes and brand assets.
- **MCP server.** WIN speaks the Model Context Protocol, so external AI agents can search companies and products through a proper interface instead of scraping the site.

### Money

- **Finance.** Your ledger: revenue, payouts, invoices, and exactly what the platform charged, per order.
- **Payments.** Cards through Stripe, with a connected account and its own payouts per seller.
- **Memberships.** Seven business tiers and three personal ones, from Free upward.
- **Multi currency.** Price and get paid in the currency that fits, with the FX margin stated per tier.

### Identity and access

- **Verified badges.** Five kinds: personal, sole proprietorship, incorporated, nonprofit and verified website. A badge is attached to the name it follows, so an employee's post never wears the company's checkmark.
- **Up to 10 workspaces** per user, each with its own team, catalogue, inbox, orders and payout setup.
- **Employees run the company account.** An owner invites people by email. They accept from their own account and act on behalf of the company, with no shared password and no lost identity. Roles are owner, admin and member, and on top of that each capability is granted individually: CRM, tasks, ERP finance, HR, projects, assets, legal, analytics, newsletters, boosting, connections, wall curation. Identity is not authority: being one of the people behind an account does not by itself let you act for it.

### The rest

- **Calendar** in month, week, day and year views, personal or company.
- **Memories.** Periodic recaps of what your company actually did.
- **Documentation hub.** 23 legal and help documents, localized, from Terms and Privacy to Fees, Payouts and Selling.
- **Settings.** Profile, account, connections, personalization, performance, security, tutorials.
- **25 guided tours**, replayable any time.
- **Accessibility.** Reduced motion support, keyboard navigation, screen reader labelling.
- **Mobile.** A real phone layout, not a squeezed desktop.

## Why We're Different

Most platforms just hook you up with contacts. WIN plugs you straight into actual opportunities that move the needle.

- **The marketplace is real.** Listings, carts, checkout, shipping, payouts and order tracking all work today, not in a future phase.
- **Selling and running the company are the same product.** Orders, CRM, finance and team all live behind one login.
- **One fee, stated plainly.** **3.2% + $0.30 per seller order**, on the subtotal and shipping, taxes excluded. That is the only amount WIN takes from your sales. Selling also requires an active paid membership, billed separately.
- **A company is a team, not a login.** Employees act for the company under their own identity, with permissions granted one capability at a time.
- **22 languages**, because a B2B network that only works in English is a regional network.
